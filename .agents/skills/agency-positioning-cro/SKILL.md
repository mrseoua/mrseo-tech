# Agency Positioning and Conversion Optimization

## Strategic position
MrSeo.tech sells business problem-solving, not generic packages or empty slogans. Targeted services: technical SEO, e-commerce SEO, Google Ads, GA4 measurement, Web Development (SaaS, CRM, API, workflow automation), Website Creation and Horoshop (UA/RU only).

## Page architecture
1. Start with target customer's concrete constraint: e.g., invisible filter landing pages, Merchant mismatch, unreliable purchase measurement, inefficient paid spend, disconnected CRM and orders.
2. Name the hidden cause and what evidence would verify it.
3. Describe exact diagnosis, proposed implementation, decision priority, deliverable and verification.
4. Show **real** portfolio projects with permission; anonymize when needed. Do not fabricate numbers, quotes, growth percentages, logos or client names.
5. Include meaningful CTA oriented around the business problem, not five repeated 'Contact us' links.
6. Localize search intent and terminology by market: UK/RU/EN/PL/DE. Check native readability; do not claim local offices, native language support or certified partnerships unless confirmed.
7. Use factual distinctions between offers; do not collapse the site's Web Development into standard website creation.

## Competitive and CRO research
- Research real competitors and keyword/SERP intent for each country before claiming differentiated expertise.
- Use Ahrefs or GSC where authorized; label simulated/unverified assumptions.
- Align page architecture with service-specific qualified lead action and GA4 form submit measurement; ensure consent/legal content is grounded.
- Use premium layout with strong typography, service-specific motion scenes, useful evidence and precise forms (website, challenge, contact).
- Define acceptance by task-completion, readability, qualified inquiry rate, event measurement and technical correctness — not 'looks expensive' alone.

## Output pattern
Provide for each planned page: audience, demand hypothesis, client problems, outcomes and evidence needs, section outline, CTA, metadata, FAQ queries, expected tracking events and outstanding validation. For actual edits use a branch/PR and run WordPress/multilingual tests.
