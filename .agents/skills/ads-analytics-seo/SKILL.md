# Paid Media, Measurement and Organic Growth

## Evidence-first approach
- Establish goal, period, currency, ad account, market, conversion action and profit economics before recommending material budget changes.
- Prefer *actual* Google Ads, GA4, Merchant Center, GSC, connected Supermetrics and Ahrefs data. Distinguish source values, attribution, modeled conversions and data gaps.
- Validate that transactions have unique IDs and amounts; review discrepancies between GA4 purchase and Google Ads conversions before comparing performance.
- Identify duplicate purchase events, mismatched currencies, incomplete consent signals, late conversions, PMax learning resets, feed price/availability errors and outlier SKUs.
- Treat ROAS, contribution margin, gross margin and true profit separately. Budget optimization must account for product margin, shipping, fees, returns and operational constraints.
- For a new client's account: baseline only first; build a prioritized plan by campaign, category and item with expected effort/risk. Do not copy another client's targets.

## Google Ads workflow
1. Audit measurement health and primary/secondary conversions.
2. Segment spend by Search, Standard Shopping, PMax, SKU/product category, brand/nonbrand, device and geography as supported.
3. Find wasted spend, disapproved items and overlap; inspect learning and conversion-delay windows.
4. Recommend explicit, measurable changes; prefer hypotheses, guardrails, holdout windows and rollback conditions.
5. **No live changes without express user approval** of the exact account and actions. Never claim a connector can mutate Google Ads unless its current permissions actually allow it.
6. Respect API quotas, change logs and data freshness. Avoid promising incremental ROAS.

## SEO workflow
- GSC by country, query, page and device; use clicks, impressions, CTR and average position with context.
- Check crawl/index status, canonicals, faceted navigation, internal linking, sitemap and localized landing page intent before publishing content.
- Prioritize commercially valuable pages over generic blog expansion when user goal is enquiries or sales.
- Keep claims aligned with verified data and explain uncertainty.

## Agency deliverables
Return a concise diagnostic table: source/period, finding, business impact, confidence, exact next action, measurement method and expected review window.
For management: produce a clear baseline, 30/60/90 day roadmap, reporting definitions and client-ready explanation.
Never leak account IDs, API keys, private client details or confidential revenue without permission. Prefer private connectors over pasted exports when supported.
