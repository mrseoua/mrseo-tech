# MrSeo.tech — Poland and Germany quality benchmark (October 2026)

## Objective
Build country-specific, high-quality service pages and a genuinely usable interactive interface. Do not claim rankings or superiority without real SERP, conversion and backlink data. Keep the entire staging site on `noindex` until it passes launch QA.

## Shipped differentiation: PL and DE
- True rotating 3D Earth on every international route (same WebGL / canvas renderer as legacy hero) with five real, language-localized service satellite links.
- Unique language-native headlines, title and meta descriptions for nine pages in each market.
- One interactive, native HTML problem → solution accordion per money page; no duplicate "what we do" list that repeats the same descriptions.
- Four concrete technical verification details for each of five commercial service pages in both countries (40 details total).
- Actual portfolio domains; no fictional growth rates, ROI, offices, client testimonials or fabricated results.
- Completed regional `hreflang` and self canonical, language selector, native contact form, 53-URL virtual international sitemap.
- Horoshop is ONLY UK / RU (both in navigation and linked pages).
- Global `noindex, nofollow` is unchanged.

## Outstanding gaps to be strictly verified before launch
1. **Actual conversion trust:** obtain permission for measured, anonymized case studies (scope, baseline, implemented solution, date range, outcome) for E-commerce SEO and Ads. Without this it is not accurate to claim to outperform established providers with documented case results.
2. **Language quality:** native Polish and German editors must review service vocabulary, naturalness, accurate business claims, legal/privacy text and inquiry process.
3. **Technical QA:** use cloud browser to verify live status codes, screenshots on desktop/mobile, rotating globe and anchors, form mail delivery, accessibility, JS performance and interactivity.
4. **Keyword research:** obtain monthly volume, difficulty, SERP composition, CPC and explicit search intent from an authorized data source. Separate Poland and Germany.
5. **Legal and communication:** confirm GDPR/privacy, invoice model, contact language and delivery process for both countries. No fake local addresses.
6. **Indexation:** leave global `noindex` on until authorized to launch. When approved, check the new XML sitemap, GSC verification and GA4 conversion capture.

## Quality acceptance checklist
- Every service page opens with a specific *business problem*, not an empty list of tools.
- Each described problem has a distinct tested solution; technical checklist does not repeat the sales copy.
- Real portfolio evidence, no guarantee of 1st position or hypothetical sales uplift.
- 3D Earth visible with a static image or Canvas fallback if WebGL fails.
- Accessible service links, keyboard-friendly accordions, reduced-motion support.
- Country-specific language, page title, meta description, canonical and bidirectional hreflang.
